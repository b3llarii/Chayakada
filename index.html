— A NOTE FROM THE COUNTER —

More than just a cup of tea

ചായയും ഓർമ്മകളും Tea & memories

Some places feel like home even when you're far away. A roadside tea shop, the clink of a glass, rain tapping on the roof — Chayakada is a small digital corner for those familiar feelings.

കേരളത്തിന്റെ ശബ്ദങ്ങൾ Sounds of Kerala

Build your own soundscape with real recordings of wind, birds, a crackling campfire, crickets, thunder and rain. Every slider is yours to mix.

നിങ്ങളുടെ സമയം Your time

Use the presets for a quick mood, set a sleep timer, or simply let the sounds run while you read, work, study or do absolutely nothing.

നിങ്ങളുടെ പാട്ടുകൾ Your songs

Bring a favourite YouTube track to the old radio. Chayakada does not host music files; playback is provided by YouTube's embedded player.

# ചായക്കട — CHAYAKADA

A little digital Kerala tea shop. Take a pause, play some ambient sounds, and enjoy the mood. ☕

## About

CHAYAKADA is a browser-based soundscape inspired by the warmth and nostalgia of a traditional Kerala chaya kada.

## Features

- Ambient sound controls for wind, birds, campfire, crickets, thunder, and rain
- Mood presets that adjust the sound levels and start playback
- YouTube radio player for music and playlists
- Sleep timer and sound volume controls
- Colour themes
- Responsive layout for desktop and mobile
- No account or sign-in required

## Credits and notes

- CHAYAKADA is an independent fan-made web project.
- YouTube playback is provided by YouTube's embedded player. Availability may depend on the video, browser, and embedding permissions.
- Audio playback may require a user interaction because of browser autoplay restrictions.
